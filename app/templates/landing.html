About Home Budget

Take Charge of Your Household Finances

Home Budget is a full-stack personal finance web application built specifically for household budgeting. It accurately records household income and expenses, categorizes all spending into meaningful groups, displays real-time monthly savings and balance calculations, and helps users stay within budget with proactive limit alerts.

Designed for clarity, simplicity, and ease of use on both phones and computers, your data is stored safely in a relational database with strict validation.
